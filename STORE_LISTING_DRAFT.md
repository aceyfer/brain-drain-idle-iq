## 3. Full description

Somewhere out there, humanity got dumber. You didn't cause it — but you're absolutely going to profit from it.

**Brain Drain: Idle IQ** is a satirical idle-clicker set in a neon corporate dystopia where intelligence is the world's scarcest resource. Tap to harvest Brain Power from a population that's forgotten how to think, then pour it into a growing empire of upgrades — from the Apex Brain Greens smoothie stand to the Brain-Rot Think Tank — each one dumber and more profitable than the last.

Every bit of Cash you launder through the Cash Investments tab (Loose Change Collective, Doomscroll Billboard, The Great Reversal, and more) gets converted into Restoration Points — funding the slow, visible healing of the world around you, stage by stage, from wasteland back to something worth living in. It's not charity. It's stolen money, coming back clean.

Guiding — or rather, narrating — the whole descent is **COGS**, your AI narrator, who roasts you, the pedestrians, and the entire premise with equal contempt while the world quietly gets better in spite of you.

Climb high enough and you'll be inducted into the **Illumisnotty** — the shadow elite you set out to take down, now with your name on the membership card. Perform "The Snotting" to give it all up and start over, stronger, richer, and no wiser.

**Features:**
- A full idle-clicker loop: tap, earn, automate, and watch the numbers climb even when you're not looking
- 16 buildings across two currencies (Brain Power and Cash), each with its own upgrade curve
- A 6-stage World Restoration system that visibly transforms the world as you spend Restoration Points
- COGS, a narrator who never lets you enjoy any of this in peace
- Prestige ("The Snotting") for permanent bonuses across runs
- A satirical corporate cyberpunk world that's more interested in roasting idiocy than glorifying it

Brain Drain: Idle IQ is built by **Eighth Kind Studios** — a small, independent team that thinks the idle genre could stand to be a little meaner to its players. In a good way.
